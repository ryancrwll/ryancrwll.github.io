<Text>I’m a Robotics Engineer specializing in Simultaneous Localization and Mapping (SLAM) and sensor fusion, with a focus on underwater and autonomous systems. I am currently pursuing my master's in the <Link color='teal.500' href='https://www.udg.edu/en/masters-en-tecnologia/mirs'>MIRS program</Link> at the Universitat de Girona's <Link color='teal.500' href='https://cirs.udg.edu/'>Underwater Vision and Robotics Research Centre (CIRS)</Link>. My thesis focuses on the underwater calibration of event-based cameras for high-dynamic range imaging. My passion is to utilize my skills and knowledge of robotics to help make the world a better place to live in for all.</Text>

<br/>

<Text>Recently, as a Side-Scan Sonar (SSS) Robotics Intern at CIRS, I developed an SSS image rectification pipeline that significantly improved feature alignment for Graph-SLAM in underwater environments. Prior to my master's, I worked as a Robotics/Automation Engineer at <Link color='teal.500' href='https://www.abdiecasting.com/about/benda-tool-model-works/'>Benda Tool & Model Works</Link>, where I architected the company's first automation department, programming ROS2, C++, and Python scripts for robotic pick-and-place movements and deploying Universal Robots for automated sanding.</Text>
